### Answer

The logs don't show why BGL keeps hitting `rts panic! - stopping execution`. In one incident it came right after a burst of network link-training failures, but that link-training pattern comes before only 4 of the 300 panic episodes. Panics are a steady weekly problem that isn't growing. They don't make the top 5 risks; the top risk is a ciod socket error.

### Answer

**In short:** on 2017-05-16, nova-compute's image-cache job failed 6 times. Each time it tried to read the disk of an instance that was being torn down, and that disk was already gone. The failure was new that day and is OpenStack's #1 risk. Across both datasets, though, BGL holds all of the top 5 risks.

The trend is daily, not hourly. The `trend` tool only buckets by day or week. The 6 run times appear under Engineer, but they come from a 12-line search, not an hourly count. All three write-ups passed `check_answer` with every claim backed, and each is on the console with its evidence.

## Engineer

**Scope:** OpenStack nova-compute on 2017-05-16 [R-0849]. Counts are lines unless stated.

**Observed**
- A full-text search for "error" that day matched 12 lines. They come in pairs: "Error during ComputeManager._run_image_cache_manager_pass" followed by "InvalidDiskInfo: … qemu-img failed". The pairs are at 03:19, 16:30, 17:41, 19:33, 20:31 and 22:16 [R-0849].
- The first error is line 45613 at 03:19:45. In the same millisecond a traceback follows, running `manage_image_cache → _list_backing_images → qemu_img_info`. It ends with "qemu-img: Could not open '/var/lib/nova/instances/16797a0f-…/disk': No such file or directory" [R-0850].
- In the 600 s before it, nova-compute logged:
  - "Deleting instance files" 15 times
  - "Unknown base file" 23 times
  - one power-state-sync warning: more instances in the database than on the hypervisor

  The whole window holds 990 lines [R-0850].
- Right after the error, the same instance 16797a0f logs that it was destroyed on the hypervisor [R-0850].

**Hypotheses**
- **The image-cache pass races with instance teardown.**
  - The power-state-sync warning came before 5 of the 8 image-cache error episodes: support 0.625, baseline 0.1163, lift 5.38, reverse lift 1.07, verdict "strong" [R-0852].
  - "Deleting instance files" came before 8 of 8 (lift 2.67, "moderate") [R-0851]. Those episodes span hours, so that support is inflated.
  - This shows the events occur together, not that one causes the other.
- **To confirm:** check whether the delete of instance 16797a0f started before 03:19:45. **To refute:** show that its disk path still existed at that time.

**Unknowns:** whether any instance or user was affected, the nova version, and whether this is a known upstream race.

**Next step:** run `trace_request` on instance 16797a0f.
