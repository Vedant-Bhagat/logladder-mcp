Three roles, same data

The role changes the rung, the tools and the next step, not just the tone.

Engineer, on linesWhat failed, and what happened just before?

search_logs → get_timeline → test_hypothesis. Highlighted log lines, anchor, precursor tested in SQL.

Next step: a check to run.

Team lead, on templates and episodesWhat keeps recurring, and whose is it?

recurring_problems → trend by team → count_by. Episodes, not lines, per owning team.

Next step: what to investigate first.

Manager, on risksWhat do we fix first?

rank_risks: a transparent score from frequency, spread, trend, severity, labels, with rank stability.

Next step: a decision, no invented cost.

Results and limits

What we measured, and what the logs can’t tell us.

Results
  • 4,955,783 lines loaded, counts match the brief, 0 rejected.
  • rts panic: 3,983 lines, only 300 episodes, steady not growing.
  • 15 / 15 questions: Claude picked the expected tools.
  • 29 / 29 receipts re-run: identical.
  • Downtime? “The logs don’t record it.”
Limits we state openly
  • Team ownership is fictional (documented).
  • Episode gap G is a choice: 10 min BGL, 60 s OpenStack; sensitivity table in docs/validation.md.
  • Templates are mined approximately (Drain3).
  • BGL is 2005–06, OpenStack 2017: “recent” means each dataset’s own last 30 days.
  • No cost, downtime or user impact in the logs, so we never claim it.
